How it works

Four steps to your results

  1. Book

    Pick a test or package and a time.

  2. Give a sample

    At the lab, or at home if you ask for it.

  3. We analyse

    Most results are ready within a day.

  4. Get your results

    Online, and shared with your doctor if you wish.

Preparation

Before your test

Good preparation avoids repeat tests.

Fasting tests

  • 8 hours without food, water is fine
  • Includes lipid, glucose and liver panels
  • Morning appointments are easiest

No-prep tests

  • Blood count, vitamin D and kidney panels
  • Eat and drink as usual

Urine sample

  • Use the sterile cup we give you
  • Midstream, first morning sample if possible

Your medication

  • Keep taking it unless your doctor says otherwise
  • Tell the lab what you take

Preparation can differ by test. We confirm it with you when you book.

FAQ

Common questions

How long until I get my results?

Most blood and urine tests are ready the same day or the next day. Some, such as hepatitis screening, are by appointment and take longer.

Do I need a doctor referral?

Not for routine tests. A referral helps for specialised tests, and your doctor can then see the results directly.

Can someone collect a sample at home?

Yes. Ask when booking and we arrange a visit.

Need a test consultation?

Not sure which test you need? Our lab team can guide you.
