FAQ

Common questions

Which medicines need a prescription?

Antibiotics, chronic-care medicines and inhalers are dispensed only with a valid prescription. Pain relief, vitamins, allergy and digestive products are available without one.

What if a medicine is out of stock?

Choose Pre-Order. We call you as soon as it arrives, and the pharmacist can suggest an equivalent if your doctor agrees.

Can the pharmacist advise me?

Yes. Ask about doses, side effects and how a medicine mixes with others you take.

Need medication advice?

Our pharmacists can help you choose the right product for your needs.
